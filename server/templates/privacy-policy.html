Privacy Policy

Seller Scan — Last updated: February 18, 2026

Introduction

Seller Scan ("we", "our", or "the app") is a product scanning and profit analysis tool for Amazon sellers. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding that information.

By using Seller Scan, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Device Identifier

We generate and store a random, anonymous device identifier on your device. This identifier is used solely to track scan usage for free-tier limits and does not contain any personally identifiable information. It cannot be used to identify you as an individual.

Scan Activity

We record the number of product scans performed per device to enforce free-tier usage limits (5 free scans). We do not store the content of your scans (product barcodes, ASINs, or search queries) on our servers.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any personal contact information
  • We do not require or support user accounts or sign-in
  • We do not collect location data
  • We do not collect photos, contacts, or other device data
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites
  • We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA)

Camera Access

Seller Scan requests access to your device's camera solely to scan product barcodes (UPC, EAN). Camera data is processed in real-time on your device to decode barcodes and is never recorded, stored, or transmitted to our servers.

How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used exclusively to:

  • Enforce free-tier scan limits per device
  • Prevent abuse of the free scanning feature
  • Maintain and improve the app's functionality

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, or profiling purposes.

Scan History

Your scan history (recently scanned products) is stored locally on your device using on-device storage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers. You can clear your scan history at any time from within the app.

Third-Party Services

Amazon Product Data

When you scan or search for a product, we query third-party data services to retrieve publicly available Amazon product information (pricing, sales rank, product details). These queries are made from our servers on your behalf and do not include any personal information about you.

Apple In-App Purchases

Subscription purchases are processed entirely through Apple's App Store and are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not process or store any payment information. Subscription status is managed locally on your device through Apple's StoreKit framework.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your information with third parties. The anonymous device identifier and scan count are stored on our secure servers solely for the purposes described above.

Data Retention

Device scan counts are retained on our servers for as long as necessary to enforce usage limits. Since no personally identifiable information is collected, there is no personal data to delete. On-device scan history can be cleared at any time by the user.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect the limited data we store. The anonymous device identifier is stored securely on your device using encrypted storage (iOS Keychain via SecureStore). Server communications are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.

Children's Privacy

Seller Scan is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since the app does not collect personal information from any user, there is no risk of inadvertent collection of children's data.

Your Rights

Because we do not collect personally identifiable information, traditional data subject rights (access, correction, deletion) do not apply in the conventional sense. However:

  • You can uninstall the app at any time to remove all locally stored data
  • You can deny or revoke camera permissions through your device settings
  • You can manage or cancel subscriptions through your Apple ID settings

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
